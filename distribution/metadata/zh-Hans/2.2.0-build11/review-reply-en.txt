Hello App Review Team,

We are submitting ClipHoook 2.2.0 (build 11). This update adds a side staging shelf for cross-app drag and drop, a global clipboard history search window, and card reordering. It also improves text editing entry points, drag previews, selection scrolling and annotation toolbar readability.

No login is required. Review notes describe each entry point, default shortcut, permission and system-version limitation. The product description, bundled privacy policy and version history have been updated for this build. It contains still-image capture and annotation, with no screen recording feature or widget extension.

Existing user-initiated automatic paste remains available with the required keyboard-event permission and focus checks; manual copy and paste are also supported. Clipboard history and staging content are stored on the Mac. The optional iPhone screenshot receiver is disabled by default and uses a paired local-network address.
